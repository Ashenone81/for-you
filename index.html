A PHILOSOPHICAL LETTER

About time,
and you.

Dear Nour,

Maybe the strange thing about time is that we spend most of our lives trying to measure it, while the moments that matter most refuse to be measured.

A minute can feel endless, and an entire evening can disappear before you even notice. I think that is what I love about spending time with you: time stops feeling like something I'm losing. It becomes something I'm actually living.

I don't need every moment with you to be extraordinary. I like the ordinary ones too — the conversations that wander nowhere, the laughs that make no sense later, the quiet moments where nothing needs to be said. Somehow, they stay.

Maybe people are not remembered by the amount of time they were in our lives, but by the way they changed the feeling of that time. And you have made some of mine softer, brighter, and much easier to remember.

So if this birthday gives me one wish, it isn't for some perfect future. It's simpler than that: I hope there are many more ordinary days where I get to say, “I'm glad I spent today with you.”

— someone who genuinely loves the time he gets to spend with you.
